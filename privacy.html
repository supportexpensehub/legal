Privacy Policy

Expense Hub is built on privacy-by-design principles and is designed to align with major global privacy frameworks including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023, India), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

Definitions

  • Personal Data: Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, as defined under applicable global data protection laws.
  • Sanitized Transaction Data: Cleaned, non-identifiable financial parameters (amount, payee name, category, and timestamp) with sensitive personal identifiers stripped.
  • Local Processing: Data extraction, parsing, and categorization performed entirely within the secure sandbox of your physical mobile device.

Prominent Disclosure & Notification Access

Expense Hub requests Notification Access permission solely to parse financial transaction alerts from authorized banking and financial applications.

  • Strict Data Filtering: The application is technically engineered to ignore all personal communications, chat messages, verification codes (OTPs), emails, and non-financial notifications. No raw notification texts are permanently logged or uploaded.
  • Full User Control: Notification Access is entirely optional. Users can enable, disable, or adjust these permissions at any time through their device settings.

Privacy-First Data Processing

To protect your sensitive financial records, we utilize a highly secure environment:

  • Secure Local Processing: The core transaction parsing and categorization engine runs entirely locally on your device. The system is not designed to access or store private message contents.
  • Cloud Syncing & Backup: If you explicitly enable cloud synchronization, only Sanitized Transaction Data is encrypted and securely backed up to our cloud databases.
  • AI-Assisted Classification: In rare cases of high ambiguity (such as an unrecognized merchant or payee), secure cloud-based AI services may be utilized to ensure data accuracy. This occurs only after removing all personal identifiers on-device.

Legal Bases for Processing (EEA Users)

For users residing in the European Economic Area (EEA), we process your data under the following GDPR legal bases:

  • Consent: For optional features like cloud account syncing and remote backups, which can be toggled off at any time.
  • Contractual Necessity: For local transaction logging and budget management operations required to compile your financial insights as requested.
  • Legitimate Interests: For analyzing fully anonymized, aggregated usage trends to optimize app stability and categorization logic.

Global Commitments & CCPA

  • No Sale or Sharing of Data: Expense Hub does not sell, rent, license, or share your Personal Data with third-party advertisers, brokers, or data syndicates for marketing or cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Local Storage Usage: We do not deploy traditional tracking cookies. We utilize secure on-device local storage (SharedPreferences) solely to preserve your application configurations and local database records.

Your Rights & Data Portability

We respect your privacy rights and provide the following controls:

  • Right to be Forgotten & Erasure: You have the right to request a complete and permanent erasure of your data. Under the GDPR, CCPA, and India's DPDP Act 2023, using the "Delete Account" option in the settings will irreversibly purge all records from both your local device and our secure cloud systems.
  • Right to Correction: You can manually edit or correct any automatically categorized transaction, payee name, or amount at any time, ensuring you retain total control over your records.
  • Data Portability: You can export a copy of your transaction history in structured CSV or Excel formats at any time using our built-in export features.
  • Right to Nominate: In accordance with the DPDP Act 2023, you have the right to nominate any other individual to exercise your data rights in the event of death or incapacity.

Security & Infrastructure

We utilize industry-leading cloud infrastructure providers for encrypted authentication and real-time database synchronization. All cloud transmissions are secured using TLS 1.3, and data is stored at rest using AES-256 encryption.

Age Restrictions

Expense Hub is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18.
