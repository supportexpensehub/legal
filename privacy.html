Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 11, 2026

Expense Hub ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Data: We only collect your email address when you sign in with Google. We do NOT collect your profile picture, contacts, or any other personal identifiers.

Financial Data: We process transaction data from your device notifications to help you track expenses. This data is stored locally on your device.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your email address solely for secure synchronization and to provide access to your Google Drive backup. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your transaction data is stored locally in an encrypted database. If you enable the Google Drive backup feature, your data is uploaded directly to your own private Google Drive storage, which we cannot access outside of the app's specific scope.

4. AI and Processing

Our "Smart AI Finance Manager" uses local and secure processing to categorize your transactions. No financial data is sent to external servers for training purposes without your explicit consent.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support.expensehub@gmail.com.
