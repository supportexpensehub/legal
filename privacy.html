Privacy Policy

Expense Hub is built on privacy-by-design principles, ensuring your financial data remains yours.

Notification Access

We use Notification Access exclusively to identify financial transactions for automatic tracking. Personal messages, OTPs, and emails are strictly ignored and never stored.

Data Processing

Processing happens in a multi-layered secure environment:

  • On-Device: 95% of analysis occurs locally using our proprietary engine.
  • Privacy Guard: Any cloud processing is performed after masking personal identifiers.
  • Zero Sale: We never sell your data to advertisers or third parties.

Data Retention

You have the Right to be Forgotten. Use the "Delete Account" option in settings to irreversibly purge your data from our systems.
