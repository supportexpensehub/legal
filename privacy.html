Privacy Policy

SpendScape is designed with privacy in mind. We aim to give you control over your financial information while collecting and processing only what is necessary to provide the features you choose to use.

This Privacy Policy explains what information SpendScape may handle, how it is used, when it may be processed by service providers, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Handle

Financial & Budget Information

When you use SpendScape, you may create, import, or enter information such as:

  • Transactions
  • Expenses and income
  • Budgets
  • Categories
  • Notes and descriptions
  • Financial reports and related records

This information is used to provide expense tracking, budgeting, reporting, analysis, and other features requested by you.

Your financial records are not sold to advertisers or data brokers.

Account Information

SpendScape can be used without an account in supported Local Mode.

If you choose to create or sign in to an account, we may receive basic account information such as your name and email address.

This information is used to authenticate your account and, where enabled, synchronize your SpendScape data across your devices.

AI-Powered Features

SpendScape may offer optional AI-powered features, including features that help you create or extract transaction information from voice input, receipt images, or other information you submit.

When you choose to use these features, the information necessary to provide the requested functionality may be processed by third-party technology providers.

AI processing is used to provide the feature you requested and is not used by SpendScape to sell your financial information.

Shared Spaces

If you choose to participate in a shared budgeting space, information that you choose to share with that space may be visible to the other members you invite.

Information that you keep outside the shared space remains separate.

Subscription Information

If you purchase SpendScape features through an app store, the purchase is processed by the applicable app-store payment system.

SpendScape receives information necessary to determine your subscription status and provide the associated features.

We do not receive your full payment-card or banking credentials through the app-store purchase process.

2. How We Use Information

We use information handled by SpendScape to:

  • Provide expense tracking and budgeting features
  • Create reports and financial summaries
  • Synchronize your information when you enable cloud features
  • Provide optional backup and restore functionality
  • Provide AI-powered features when you request them
  • Manage subscriptions and access to paid features
  • Maintain and secure the service
  • Respond to support or privacy requests
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell your personal financial records, transaction history, budgets, or similar financial information to advertisers or data brokers.

3. Local Mode & Cloud Features

Local Mode

SpendScape can be used without creating an account in Local Mode.

When using Local Mode, your financial records are stored on your device and are not synchronized to a SpendScape cloud account.

Cloud Synchronization

If you choose to sign in and enable synchronization, information associated with your SpendScape account may be securely synchronized through our service infrastructure.

Cloud synchronization is optional.

Backup & Restore

SpendScape may provide optional backup and restore functionality.

If you choose to enable backup, the information required to create and restore your SpendScape backup may be stored using a third-party cloud storage service associated with your account.

You control whether you use this optional functionality.

4. AI, Voice & Receipt Scanning

Some SpendScape features may use artificial intelligence to assist with tasks such as:

  • Converting voice input into transactions
  • Reading information from receipts
  • Extracting transaction details
  • Assisting with categorization or other financial-entry features

When you actively use one of these features, the information required to perform the requested operation may be transmitted to third-party processing services.

We aim to limit the information sent to what is reasonably necessary for the requested feature.

Receipt images, audio, and other inputs submitted for AI processing are handled according to the applicable processing configuration and provider terms.

SpendScape does not use your financial records to sell advertising.

5. Third-Party Services

SpendScape may rely on trusted third-party service providers to provide certain functionality, including:

  • Account and cloud services
  • AI-powered functionality
  • Optional cloud backup
  • Subscription management
  • Advertising
  • App distribution and payment processing

These providers may process information necessary to provide their respective services.

We require our service providers to handle information in accordance with their applicable terms and privacy requirements.

Third-party providers may have their own privacy policies governing their processing of information.

SpendScape does not permit third-party service providers to use your financial records for purposes inconsistent with the functionality for which they are engaged.

6. Advertising

SpendScape may display advertisements through third-party advertising services.

Advertising providers may process information such as device information, advertising identifiers, and other information permitted under applicable privacy settings to:

  • Deliver advertisements
  • Measure advertising performance
  • Prevent fraud
  • Improve advertising services

Your transaction history, budgets, and financial records are not provided to advertisers for advertising purposes.

You may also have advertising controls available through your device or applicable platform settings.

7. Information Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information handled by SpendScape against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration.

Where information is transmitted to our online services, appropriate security measures are used to protect the transmission.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. However, we take reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the information we handle.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

Local Data

Information stored locally remains on your device until you delete it, remove the app, or otherwise clear the applicable application data.

You are responsible for maintaining backups of information that you choose to keep only locally.

Cloud Data

If you use an account or cloud synchronization, associated information may remain in our systems while your account is active or as necessary to provide the requested services.

Account Deletion

If you choose to delete your SpendScape account, you may request deletion of the associated account and cloud data through the available account settings or deletion process.

We will delete applicable information from our active systems within a reasonable period, subject to information that we are required or permitted to retain for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or other legitimate purposes.

Deleting your account does not necessarily delete information stored independently by third-party services under your own accounts, such as optional backups.

9. Your Privacy Choices & Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including the right to:

  • Access information associated with your account
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete applicable information
  • Request a copy or export of your information
  • Restrict or object to certain processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Raise a privacy concern or complaint with the appropriate authority

You may also control whether you use optional features such as cloud synchronization, backup, shared spaces, and AI-powered functionality.

To exercise applicable rights, contact us using the information provided below.

Additional rights may apply depending on your country or region.

10. Children's Privacy

SpendScape is intended for individuals who are 18 years of age or older, or the applicable age of majority in their jurisdiction.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to SpendScape, our services, or applicable legal requirements.

When we make changes, we will update the “Last Updated” date at the top of this policy.

Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent for material changes.
