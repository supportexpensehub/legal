Privacy Policy

Expense Hub is built using privacy-by-design principles and is intended to align with major global privacy frameworks including GDPR, DPDP (India), and CCPA (California).

1

Notification Access Permission

Expense Hub uses the Notification Access permission exclusively to identify financial transaction notifications for automatic expense tracking. Non-financial notifications, personal conversations, OTPs, emails, and private messages are ignored and not stored. The app is designed to process only transaction-related notifications from recognized financial institutions and is not intended to process personal messages or non-financial communications.

User Control: Users can disable Notification Access or cloud synchronization at any time through device or app settings.

2

Privacy-First Processing

To ensure maximum privacy and data security, we utilize a multi-layered approach to data refinement:

  • Secure Local Processing: The vast majority of transaction data is processed entirely on your device. The system is not designed to access or store private message contents.
  • Local Intelligence: The app utilizes local behavioral patterns to refine categorization over time. This learning occurs exclusively within your device's secure environment.
  • AI-Assisted Categorization: In limited scenarios of high ambiguity (such as unrecognized merchant codes), secure cloud-based AI services may be utilized to ensure data accuracy. This occurs only after local masking of sensitive personal identifiers.
3

Data Minimization & Cloud Sync

In alignment with "Privacy by Design" principles, only "Sanitized Transaction Data" (Amount, Payee, Category, Date) is synchronized to the cloud if you enable sync. Raw notification text is never uploaded or stored outside your device.

4

Right to be Forgotten (Data Deletion)

You have the right to request a complete and irreversible purge of your data from our cloud servers and your local device. Deletion timelines may vary by region to comply with mandatory local security and audit laws.

To request account or data deletion: Use the "Delete Account" option within the Profile settings of the Expense Hub app, or contact us at the email address provided below.

5

Right to Correction

We provide all users with the "Right to Correction". You can manually override any automated category or brand detection at any time, helping ensure your financial records remain accurate and user-controlled.

6

Data Portability

You have the right to request a copy of your transaction data in a structured, machine-readable format. Our Export feature (CSV, Excel) allows you to take your data with you at any time.

7

Third-Party Service Providers

Expense Hub utilizes a limited number of trusted third-party service providers for essential functions:

  • Cloud Infrastructure: Used for secure user authentication, real-time data synchronization, and encrypted cloud storage for receipt backups. All data is encrypted using industry-standard protocols (TLS 1.3 in transit, AES-256 at rest).
  • Intelligent Processing: Secure AI services power our "Smart Insights" and scanning features. Data is handled in accordance with industry-standard security and privacy practices.
8

Age Restrictions

Expense Hub is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.
